# Isaac Dobson Portfolio

A personal portfolio presented as if visitors are using a classic black-and-white Macintosh. It introduces Isaac's software engineering work while staying readable and easy to navigate.

## Experience

**Intro**:
The opening sequence in which a 3D compact Macintosh boots in a dark room lit only by its screen, the view circles in and through the screen, and Isaac's name and Headline are typed.
_Avoid_: Splash screen, loader, Lottie

**Short Boot**:
The abbreviated Intro, without the 3D Macintosh: a brief screen power-on shown to returning visitors and to visitors whose browsers can't show the full Intro.
_Avoid_: Mini intro

**Mac Desktop**:
The Macintosh's screen as the visitor's whole screen once the Intro ends: the Menu Bar along the top and a 1-bit pattern behind the Mac Window.
_Avoid_: Background, wallpaper, desktop (alone, which suggests a kind of computer)

**Mac Window**:
The classic window on the Mac Desktop where portfolio content is read and scrolled.
_Avoid_: Modal, card, page

**Menu Bar**:
The persistent classic-Mac menu strip used to move between Sections, reach the Easter Egg, and turn the Startup Chime on or off.
_Avoid_: Navbar, header

**Section**:
One of the portfolio's major content areas: About, Projects, Technologies, or Contact.
_Avoid_: Page, tab

**Time Machine**:
The lens and Full color toggle that reveal the full-color source behind a 1-bit image. Images in one project figure can change together as a group.
_Avoid_: Hover effect, color mode, original (which means "not Apple-owned" elsewhere)

**Startup Chime**:
The original sound that accompanies the Intro or Short Boot once a visitor turns sound on. Sound is off until they do.
_Avoid_: Boot sound, Mac chime

**CRT Effects**:
The faint grain, scanlines, dimmed edges suggesting a curved tube, and flicker drawn over the Mac Desktop. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see them held still.
_Avoid_: Filter, overlay

**Classic Pointer**:
The original 1-bit arrow shown in place of the system pointer on computers with a mouse or trackpad.
_Avoid_: Custom cursor

**Easter Egg**:
The hidden About This Mac window that describes Isaac with playful computer-style specs.
_Avoid_: Secret page

## Content

**Headline**:
The professional positioning shown with Isaac's name: "Software Engineer — Data & Platform."
_Avoid_: Tagline, subtitle

**Case Study**:
A project write-up that explains the problem, approach, architecture, and outcome.
_Avoid_: Project card

**Public Resume**:
The downloadable resume version with the personal phone number removed.
_Avoid_: CV, full resume
