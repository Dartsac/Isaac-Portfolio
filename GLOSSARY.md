# Isaac Dobson Portfolio

A personal portfolio presented as if visitors are using a classic black-and-white Macintosh. It introduces Isaac's software engineering work while staying readable and easy to navigate.

## Experience

**Intro**:
The opening sequence in which a 3D compact Macintosh boots in a dark room lit only by its screen, the view swings round to face that screen and moves in until it fills the visitor's, the screen crossfades into the Mac Desktop, and Isaac's name and Headline are typed.
_Avoid_: Splash screen, loader, Lottie

**Short Boot**:
The abbreviated Intro, without the 3D Macintosh: a brief screen power-on shown to returning visitors and to visitors whose browsers can't show the full Intro.
_Avoid_: Mini intro

**Mac Desktop**:
The Macintosh's screen as the visitor's whole screen once the Intro ends: the Menu Bar along the top and a 1-bit pattern behind the Mac Window.
_Avoid_: Background, wallpaper, desktop (alone, which suggests a kind of computer)

**Mac Window**:
The classic window on the Mac Desktop where portfolio content is read and scrolled.
_Avoid_: Modal, card, page

**Menu Bar**:
The persistent classic-Mac menu strip used to move between Sections, reach the Easter Egg, Restart where there is room, and turn Dark Mode and the Startup Chime on or off.
_Avoid_: Navbar, header

**Restart**:
The control that plays the full Intro again, as on a first visit, from the start of the portfolio. It is offered in the Menu Bar where there is room, and at the end of the Mac Window on every screen, to visitors who don't prefer reduced motion.
_Avoid_: Replay button, rewatch

**Dark Mode**:
The Mac Desktop drawn inverted, white on black, as a 1-bit screen can be, through the Intro as well. It follows the visitor's system setting until they turn it on or off themselves, a choice remembered for later visits. Its control is in the Menu Bar on every screen. 1-bit images keep their own drawing, black on white.
_Avoid_: Theme, night mode, inverted colors

**Section**:
One of the portfolio's major content areas: About, Projects, Technologies, or Contact.
_Avoid_: Page, tab

**Time Machine**:
The lens and Full color toggle that reveal the full-color source behind a 1-bit image. Images in one project figure can change together as a group. Full color forwards an image to present-day viewing, crisp, with no CRT Effects over it; a peek through the lens stays under them.
_Avoid_: Hover effect, color mode, original (which means "not Apple-owned" elsewhere)

**Startup Chime**:
The original sound that accompanies the Intro or Short Boot once a visitor turns sound on. Sound is off until they do.
_Avoid_: Boot sound, Mac chime

**CRT Effects**:
The faint grain, scanlines, and dimmed edges suggesting a curved tube drawn over the Mac Desktop, except over images the Time Machine shows in full color. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see them held still.
_Avoid_: Filter, overlay

**Classic Pointer**:
The original 1-bit arrow shown in place of the system pointer on computers with a mouse or trackpad.
_Avoid_: Custom cursor

**Easter Egg**:
The hidden About This Mac window that describes Isaac with playful computer-style specs.
_Avoid_: Secret page

## Content

**Headline**:
The professional positioning shown with Isaac's name: "Software Engineer — Data & Platform."
_Avoid_: Tagline, subtitle

**Case Study**:
A project write-up that explains the problem, approach, architecture, and outcome.
_Avoid_: Project card

**Public Resume**:
The downloadable resume version with the personal phone number removed.
_Avoid_: CV, full resume
